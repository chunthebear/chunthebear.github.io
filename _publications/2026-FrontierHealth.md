When someone gets a brain injury, they often struggle with mental health or drug and alcohol problems at the same time. Doctors want to help, but it is not always clear which treatments work best when a patient is dealing with both of these issues at once.

To figure this out, we looked at over 600 past studies involving more than 800,000 people. We found that most of the research out there focuses on strokes, depression, and using medication. However, we also noticed that many of these past studies were not very high quality.

To make this massive amount of information useful, we built an interactive online map. Now, doctors can use this map to look up the best ways to treat their patients, and other scientists can use it to see what topics still need to be studied.
